# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pickle to be a friendly, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, or anything else.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on the project and on the work, not on the person.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or jokes
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussion
- Publishing other people's private information
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem, email **getafterword@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
